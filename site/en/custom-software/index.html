OUR PROCESS

A visible project process that reduces uncertainty.

  1. 1

    Requirement analysis

    We talk through how the work runs today, which step burdens whom, and what will be measured.

  2. 2

    Scope and quote

    We write the work up as a defined scope and present it with a timeline and a price. What is out of scope is written down too.

  3. 3

    Development

    We deliver in pieces and you see each piece working. Changing direction at an interim delivery is possible.

  4. 4

    Go-live

    Installation, data migration, user training and the switch to production.

  5. 5

    Support

    Bug fixing, updates and new requests. An ongoing relationship, not a one-off delivery.
